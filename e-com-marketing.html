Phase 1: Meta Ads strategy and setup

We start with 1 to 3 distinct campaigns to test audiences, build pixel data and drive bottom-of-funnel conversions. Pick a campaign to see how it works.

Campaign 1

Top of funnel: broad and interest-based

Reach women aged 18 to 45 in tier 1 and tier 2 Indian cities interested in ethnic wear, fashion accessories, wedding planning and competitor jewellery brands.

Campaign 2

Advantage+ Shopping Campaign

Meta's AI tests creatives and audiences automatically. The focus is entirely on driving purchases from high-intent buyers.

Campaign 3

Bottom of funnel: retargeting

Dynamic product ads reach people who added items to their cart or engaged with your Instagram page but did not purchase.

Ad creatives that let the jewellery shine

For jewellery, aesthetics dictate conversion. We recommend mixing three formats.

The try-on video

A 15-second Reel of a model wearing the piece in natural light, to show scale and shine.

Dynamic carousels

Top-selling collections such as "Kundan Sets Under ₹1999" and "Everyday Office Wear", linked straight to product pages.

Premium flat lays

High-quality stills of the jewellery styled with traditional fabrics or minimal aesthetic props.

Complimentary consultation

Phase 2: influencer marketing

Influencers provide the social proof needed to sell fashion accessories online. With an accessible ticket size, we suggest a micro and nano-influencer strategy.

Target profiles

Indian fashion, lifestyle and ethnic wear creators with 10k to 50k followers. Their audiences are highly engaged and acquisition costs are lower.

Collaboration model

Barter (gifting the jewellery) plus a small base payout depending on average reel reach.

Deliverables per creator

  • 1 Instagram Reel
    "Styling my favourite kurti with [Brand Name] jewellery" or "Festive jewellery haul under ₹2000"
  • 2 Story integrations
    With a direct link to the product page

Creator tiers: from nano to celebrity

Indicative Instagram rates in India per Reel or post. Bigger audiences cost far more while engagement tends to fall, which is why we lead with nano and micro creators for ₹500 to ₹5,000 products.

Rates vary by niche, content format and usage rights. Nano creators may accept barter or lower-tier deals from ₹500 to ₹3,000. Some macro niches quote well above the range shown. Treat all figures as planning estimates, not quotes.
